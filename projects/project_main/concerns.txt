## HIGH
1. SELL qty ignores actual holdings — FIXED
   - Was: sells sized to budget/price, could exceed position
   - Fix: build_order_plan takes positions {ticker: qty} (fetched via
     AlpacaClient.get_positions in cli._components, threaded through
     graph.run_pipeline); SELL qty = min(budget/price, held qty), skipped
     when nothing held. SELL notional no longer consumes the BUY-side cap.

2. No volatility scaling (equal $ ≠ equal risk) — FIXED
   - Was: $100 per ticker regardless of realized vol
   - Fix: generate_signal emits daily_vol (std of last 20 daily returns);
     budget *= min(1.0, TARGET_DAILY_VOL / daily_vol). Scale-down only:
     low-vol names never exceed the base budget.

## MEDIUM
3. No conviction weighting — FIXED (quant confidence)
   - Was: binary approve/veto → equal budget
   - Fix: budget *= clamp(quant.confidence / 0.95, 0.25, 1.0). Debate
     winner weighting: see item 10 (completed 2026-09-07).

4. No sector/correlation concentration cap — FIXED (sector cap)
   - Was: 10 tech tickers = 100% sector exposure
   - Fix: fundamentals now map yfinance "sector"; graph derives a
     sector_map per run; BUY notional per sector capped at
     equity * MAX_SECTOR_PCT (default 0.30); sectorless tickers exempt.
     Correlation concentration: see item 11 (completed 2026-09-07).

5. PDT gate blocks SELL only — FIXED (gate inverted)
   - Was: SELLs blocked during restriction → trapped positions, long bias
   - Fix: inverted. Restriction blocks BUYs (risk-adding) and allows
     SELLs (risk-reducing exits).

7. Kelly / Kelly-fraction sizing (needs backtest stats) — FIXED

     `python -m src.cli backtest --tickers ... --period 2y --horizon 5`)
     replays the quant signals over history (no LLM) and writes
     win-rate/payoff/Kelly stats to data/backtest_stats.json
     (BACKTEST_STATS_PATH). build_order_plan multiplies budgets by
     1 + (KELLY_MAX_MULT - 1) * min(fraction / KELLY_FRACTION_CAP, 1)

     The cumulative BUY cap stays at equity. Absent/junk stats and
     non-positive fractions leave sizing untouched (factor 1.0).

13. Quant signal uses price only (no volume / regime confirmation)

      OHLCV frame so the volume gate works in backtests" was true for
      close-mode replays but NOT for the overnight replay — it rebuilt

15. Pairs legs could bypass the single-leg caps (concentration escape)
    — ADDRESSED BY DESIGN (2026-09-12, pairs module)
    - Fix: pair legs are planned INSIDE build_order_plan
      (_build_pair_order_plans) and consume the SAME cumulative equity
      cap, sector cap and correlation-cluster cap accumulators as the
      single-leg plan, plus two extra rails of their own: per-pair
      (equity * MAX_PAIR_PCT = 3%, BOTH legs combined) and total-pairs
      (equity * MAX_PAIRS_PCT = 30%). Cap priority is explicit: pair

      equity cap binds, pair legs are skipped first (the single-leg
      edge carries the validated Kelly stats; pairs are unvalidated).
      Pair BUY legs also pass the same PDT + panic gates.

16. Pairs exits could strand partial residues (conviction-scaled exits)
    — ADDRESSED BY DESIGN (2026-09-12)
    - Risk: the plan sketched SELL_EXIT qty = min(scaled_budget/price,
      held_qty); with conviction = clamp(|z|/z_entry, 0.25, 1.0) an exit
      that fires at |z| < z_exit would scale its own budget to ~25% and
      only ever partially exit, leaving a residue that re-exits dust-
      sized forever (and the dust filter would then skip it entirely).
    - Fix: SELL_EXIT legs are MECHANICAL full-quantity exits of held qty
      (no conviction/vol/regime/cost scaling) — the exit fires because
      the spread reverted, not because of edge strength; same precedent
      as overnight exits. Unheld legs emit nothing (never a short).
